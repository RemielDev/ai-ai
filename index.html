Two hotkeys. Four providers. Zero proxy.

Bring your own key from Gemini, OpenAI, OpenRouter, or Anthropic. Prompts go straight from your machine to the provider.

Ctrl+Shift+Space

Reads Claude's last response, generates 3-6 follow-up prompts in a floating overlay above the chat input.

Ctrl+Shift+Enter

While typing in Claude, rewrites your draft in place as a clearer, more specific prompt.

4 providers, your pick

Gemini Flash (~$0.0001/call), OpenAI, OpenRouter free tier, or Anthropic. Switch with one dropdown.

Rate-limited & private

60 calls/min hard cap. Keys in Windows Credential Manager. No telemetry without your opt-in.

Free forever.

MIT licensed. 2.3 MB installer. Works with any Anthropic-compatible Claude Desktop conversation.
